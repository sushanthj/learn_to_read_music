How to use:

1. Select note duration and accidental (if needed)

2. Left click on the staff to add notes, right click to delete

3. On touch devices: tap to add, double tap to delete

4. Drag notes up/down to change their pitch

5. Your composition auto-saves and will be available in Practice mode
